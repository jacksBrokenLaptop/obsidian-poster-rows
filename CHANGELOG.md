## 0.1.0

First release: a Bases view that shows your notes like a streaming service.

- A featured banner that cycles through your top-rated notes with covers.
- Rows of cover cards you scroll sideways. Each group becomes a row when the view is grouped; otherwise you get Recently modified, Top rated and one row per value of a property you pick.
- Select a card to open the note, or show a details card first with the cover, rating, description and properties.
- Options for the cover, title, rating and rating scale, banner text, card width and cover shape. Square art such as album covers is letterboxed instead of cropped.
- Works on desktop and mobile, in light and dark themes.

Requires Obsidian 1.10.0 or newer.
